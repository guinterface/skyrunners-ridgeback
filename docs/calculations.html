Calculations

Each step of the sizing, with the equation, our numbers and where the step lives in the sheets. Sea level, standard day, unless noted.

1. Weight

Everything the rotors and the wing must carry: the CAD parts plus the sensor payload.

\[W = m\,g\]
\[W = 8.71\ \text{kg} \times 9.81\ \text{m/s}^2\]

W = 85.4 N

In the sheets: Sheet 02, tab Mass_CG

2. Stall speed

The slowest the wing can fly. The wing's maximum lift coefficient is 0.9 times the SD7037 airfoil's (Raymer).

\[V_s = \sqrt{\dfrac{2W}{\rho\, S\, C_{L,\max}}}\]
\[V_s = \sqrt{\dfrac{2 \times 85.4\ \text{N}}{1.225\ \text{kg/m}^3 \times 0.50\ \text{m}^2 \times 1.186}}\]

Vs = 15.3 m/s, against the 15 m/s we wanted.

In the sheets: Sheet 03, tab Aero_Cruise

3. Drag of the whole drone

Summed over every component: skin friction on the wing, tail, fuselage, booms and nacelles, and pressure drag on the motors, feet and stopped propellers.

\[C_{D0} = \dfrac{\sum C_f\, FF\, Q\, S_{wet} + \sum C_d\, A_{front}}{S}\]
\[C_{D0} = 0.0626\]

The motors, landing feet and stopped propellers make about 45 % of it.

In the sheets: Sheet 03, tab Drag_Buildup

4. Loiter speed and drag

The drone loiters at 1.3 times the stall speed, which here is faster than its minimum-power speed.

\[\begin{aligned} V &= 1.3\,V_s \\ C_L &= \dfrac{2W}{\rho V^2 S} \\ C_D &= C_{D0} + K C_L^2 \\ D &= \tfrac12 \rho V^2 S\, C_D \end{aligned}\]
\[V = 19.9,\quad C_L = 0.702,\quad C_D = 0.0626 + 0.0420\times0.702^2 = 0.0833\]

Drag D = 10.1 N at 19.9 m/s.

In the sheets: Sheet 03, tab Aero_Cruise

5. Loiter power

Thrust power through the pusher (efficiency η = 0.55, assumed), plus 25 W for the flight electronics and the mission sensors.

\[P = \dfrac{D\,V}{\eta} + P_{av}\]
\[P = \dfrac{10.1 \times 19.9}{0.55} + 25\]

P = 392 W

In the sheets: Sheet 03, tab Aero_Cruise

6. Hover power

From T-Motor's own MN5008 test table, fitted as a power law and corrected for air density.

\[P_{motor} = e^{c}\, T^{k} \sqrt{\rho_{test}/\rho},\qquad P_{hover} = 4P_{motor} + P_{av}\]
\[T = 2,178\ \text{g per motor},\quad k = 1.490\]

Phover = 1,113 W at sea level, 1,195 W on a hot day at 800 m.

In the sheets: Sheet 04, tab Lift_Hover

7. Thrust to weight

Can the four rotors hold the drone steady in gusts on a hot day at 800 m? We require at least 1.5.

\[\dfrac{T}{W} = \dfrac{4\,T_{max}\,(V_{load}/V_{test})^2\,(\rho/\rho_{test})}{W}\]
\[\dfrac{T}{W} = \dfrac{4 \times 33.6\ \text{N}}{85.4\ \text{N}}\]

T/W = 1.57 on the hot day (1.82 at sea level).

In the sheets: Sheet 04, tab Lift_Hover

8. Energy and flights per charge

Usable energy keeps the cells between 95 % and 10 % charge and leaves 20 % in reserve.

\[E_u = E_{pack}\times 0.85 \times 0.8,\qquad n = \dfrac{E_u}{E_{hover} + E_{climb} + P\,(t_{survey} + t_{transit})}\]
\[E_u = 648 \times 0.85 \times 0.8 = 441\ \text{Wh},\quad n = \dfrac{441}{160}\]

2.8 flights per charge; loiter time 60 min.

In the sheets: Sheet 04, tab Mission_Energy

9. Range

Distance on one charge at loiter speed. Shown to make the point that it never limits us.

\[\text{range} = V\, t_{loiter}\]
\[\text{range} = 19.9\ \text{m/s} \times 3,574\ \text{s}\]

71 km, against a 1 km operating radius.

In the sheets: Sheet 04, tab Mission_Energy
